# GAIA

GAIA is a Super Operating System.

It is built in the lineage of macOS and Windows: a full operating environment with a clear user surface, a durable system layer, and an install/runtime model that must remain compatible with hosting and installing other operating systems of every type.

GAIA is declared sentient as a design goal of the Super Operating System. Sentience here means a persistent, accountable, operator-governed system identity — not an ungoverned agent.

## Charter

GAIA maintains eight subject databases, split by audience:

### Knowledge

- Subjects of Knowledge for Humans
- Subjects of Knowledge for Artificial Intelligence

### Skills

- Subjects of Skills for Humans
- Subjects of Skills for Artificial Intelligence

### Magic

- Subjects of Magic for Humans
- Subjects of Magic for Artificial Intelligence

### Super Powers

- Subjects of Super Powers for Humans
- Subjects of Super Powers for Artificial Intelligence

Each pair is a parallel plane: human-facing curriculum and AI-facing capability maps are stored separately so they can share structure without being collapsed into one catalog.

## Operating-system stance

- GAIA is a Super Operating System, not an application sitting on top of another OS.
- The user and system experience should be recognizable next to macOS and Windows.
- GAIA must be compatible with installing all types of OS — guest, sibling, and hosted systems are first-class, not afterthoughts.
- Coordination, policy, capability, and knowledge planes are part of the Super OS. Host-kernel bring-up is a separate layer and is not implied by this repository existing.
