Sibling of [Basic Health & Hygiene](../basic-health-and-hygiene/README.md) (especially that subject's M04). Not a merge. Not a clinic. Not a meal prescription.

This folder is inside the Human Knowledge catalog. It is not a new plane.

Nutrition taught as calorie arithmetic produces recall without food sense. GAIA treats Basic Nutrition as how food becomes body, brain, culture, and landscape — fifteen modules. Guidelines are labeled corpora, not a house religion.

US Dietary Guidelines, EAT-Lancet planetary diet, Mediterranean / MIND / DASH, and named traditional patterns sit side by side. Where they conflict (protein and animal foods vs planetary limits), the conflict stays visible. "Eat real food" is a slogan, not a law of nature.

Food logs, genotypes, and microbiome reports are vault-scoped. Eating-disorder recovery is refer-out. Weight is not a moral rank.

## Science the system is bound to

Macros and micros enable physiology. Patterns beat isolated superfoods. Ultra-processed (NOVA 4) is a classification to learn.

Brain energy demand, structural fats, transmitter precursors. Fiber to SCFA; gut–brain as literacy. Emerging claims stay labeled emerging.

Two public frames stay on stage: labeled US DGA versions, and EAT-Lancet planetary diet. Precision nutrition is research literacy. GAIA does not invent a metabolic twin from chat.
