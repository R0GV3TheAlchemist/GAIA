Sociology taught as a list of social facts produces recall without structure. GAIA treats Basic Sociology as how groups organize, reproduce, change, and sort power and chance — sixteen modules.

Three classical perspectives get equal classroom time: functionalism, conflict, symbolic interactionism. Later lenses (feminist, critical race, queer, postcolonial, network, rational choice) are tools, not a house creed. Intersectionality is an analytic lens among others. Inequality and measured social progress both stay on the page.

GAIA does not rank parties, run a movement, or pick a revolution. Crime modules are literacy, not a how-to. Digital modules do not surveil users.

## Science the system is bound to

Functionalism explains persistence. Conflict explains distribution and rupture. Interactionism explains everyday meaning. Used alone, each fails. A complete sociology explains durable inequality and genuine gains (literacy, longevity, rights expansions) without treating either as the whole story.

Mills: personal trouble ↔ public issue. That move is the skill. It is not a mandate to treat every trouble as only structure or only character.
