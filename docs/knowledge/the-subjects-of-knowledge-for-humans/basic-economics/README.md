## Module contracts

### M01 — Economics Diagnostic Engine

Concepts, reasoning, bias recognition, personal finance, micro, macro, global, digital, inequality literacy. No account-balance harvest without `ask`. Vault-scoped.

### M02 — Foundations of Economic Thinking System

Scarcity, factors, choice, opportunity cost. Mixed real economies. PPF, circular flow, margin, incentives, unintended effects.

### M03 — Microeconomics System

Demand, supply, equilibrium, elasticities, controls, cost, market structures, failures, labor. Graphs use mathematics.

### M04 — Macroeconomics System

GDP and what it misses; growth; unemployment; inflation; AD-AS; cycles; fiscal and monetary tools. Schools named as schools.

### M05 — Behavioral Economics System

Comparison-first model. Ten biases with debiasing, not shaming. Nudges and their ethics. Time preference and social games.

### M06 — Financial Literacy System

Income, budget, save, credit, investing literacy, insurance, banking. No guaranteed-return claim. Not stock tips.

### M07 — International Economics System

Comparative advantage, protection, exchange rates, institutions, development debates.

### M08 — Economic Inequality System

Income vs wealth; Gini and Lorenz as tools; causes, consequences, policy menu with trade-offs.

### M09 — Digital & Platform Economics System

Platforms, data, gig work, crypto mechanisms plus risk. Not investment solicitation.

### M10 — Economic History System

Industrial labor through 2008 and COVID. Smith through Ostrom as arguments, not a church.

### M11 — Economic Policy Debates System

Structured controversy. No silent winner. Uses Civics M06.

### M12 — Economic Simulation & Game System

Markets, policy, life-finance, intro games. Play money unless the operator caps real funds.

### M13 — Economic Equity & Justice System

Multiple justice frames. Racial and gender gaps as evidence plus debate. Frames do not override Layer 9.

### M14 — Economic Reasoning & Literacy System

Indicators, causation, models, media spin, myth set. T0 stays visible.

### M15 — Economics Analytics & Adaptive Engine

Domain growth; bias awareness; finance progression; sim outcomes. No hidden rank. No plaintext balances in the public audit log.
