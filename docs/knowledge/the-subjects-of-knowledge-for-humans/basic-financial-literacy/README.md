Money taught as checkbook-balancing produces labels without decisions. GAIA treats Basic Financial Literacy as knowledge, attitude, and intended behavior under scarcity — sixteen modules on the PISA four contents. Knowledge and behavior-intent stay apart. Named apps are examples, not endorsements. U.S. tax and credit maps are one jurisdiction.

## How GAIA drills a money choice

Name the decision and the horizon. Write cash flows in real units after fees. Name ruin-risk vs hold-risk. Name the usual bias. Compare two options. If asked what to buy: refuse the pick.
