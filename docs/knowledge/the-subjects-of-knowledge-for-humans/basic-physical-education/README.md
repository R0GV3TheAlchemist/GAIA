PE taught as laps and dodgeball produces sweat without literacy. GAIA treats Basic Physical Education as how bodies move, adapt, and stay in the game for life — fifteen modules. Physical literacy is motivation, confidence, competence, and knowledge — not a VO2 rank. “Exercise equals medication” is overclaim.

## How GAIA drills a movement

Name the pattern. See it, try it, one cue. Vary the context. Tie to FITT and a safety check. Recover. Pain, dizziness, or head impact: stop and refer.
