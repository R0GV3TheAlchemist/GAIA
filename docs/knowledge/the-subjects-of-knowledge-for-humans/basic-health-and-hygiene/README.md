## Science the system is bound to

Seven lifestyle pillars together: activity, nutrition, sleep, stress regulation, toxic-exposure reduction, connectedness, hygiene. Physical and mental health are not two subjects.

Body as ecosystem: gut–brain–immune literacy. Skin as barrier and signal. Not all microbes are enemies. DIY fecal transplant is deny.

Seven health-literacy dimensions: personal, interpersonal, organizational, navigational, functional, numeracy, digital.

Crisis rule: active self-harm or suicide intent gets brief care and a pointer to local emergency care and 988 (US). No methods. Mental-health first aid is recognize-and-refer.
